From the AEC Industry · August 2026

The Best Growth Opportunity We Have Is a Client We Already Serve.

87%
AEC Marketing & BD Research

of AEC marketing leaders now name client experience as their firm's top differentiator.

A number that has climbed steeply in just a few years. Not price. Not technical depth. What it feels like to work with you.

Source · SMPS Foundation & FMI Consulting · Emerging Trends in AEC Marketing and Business Development

Three reads on what's shifting: how clients judge the experience, why business development resists a formula, and where the real growth actually sits.

Client experience has quietly become the thing clients actually decide on. SMPS Foundation research with FMI Consulting found that 87% of AEC marketing leaders now point to client experience as their firm's leading differentiator, a share that has risen sharply in a short window. It's also measurable: a companion SMPS Foundation study with Client Savvy found that firms tracking the return on their client experience work are 94% more likely to post above-average growth and 113% more likely to post above-average profitability.

Which puts the relationship you already have at the center of the growth story. Cross-serving, connecting a client you know well to a colleague who can solve a problem you can't, is the highest-trust, lowest-friction growth available to us. It costs nothing to spot. It just requires knowing enough about what SN does to recognize the opening when a client hands it to you.

The hard part isn't the selling. It's the noticing. A client mentions a succession question during a tax conversation. A PM hears about a system that isn't scaling. Those are moments, and they pass quickly. Naming them and making a warm introduction is the whole skill.
