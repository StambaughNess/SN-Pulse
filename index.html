Feature Spotlights
Strategic Firm Announcement · September 2026

The SN and Zweig Group Merger: A New Kind of Firm for a New Era in AEC.

This week, Steve and Chad shared news that will shape the next chapter for all of us. Here's what was announced, where to find answers, and what comes next.

Stambaugh Ness and Zweig Group are merging. The goal isn't size for its own sake. It's a platform that brings together the expertise, insight, and capabilities AEC leaders need to build stronger firms, and it's designed to help the firms we serve create lasting value and shape what's next in the industry.

An announcement of this size brings excitement and pride, and naturally, a lot of questions. If you couldn't attend live, the recording is ready for you now. The SNZweig One FAQs cover the questions we've heard most so far, and frequent internal communications will keep the updates coming. For our Zweig Group colleagues joining SN, the October 5 Live Orientation will answer every remaining question.

One ask while we get there: please keep this news inside the firm. Select clients will hear it from us on September 14, and the public announcement follows on September 15.

"Every person on this team plays a critical role in bringing this new firm to life. Your expertise, energy, relationships, and commitment are what will make this vision real."
Steve Hake and Chad Clinehens

Systems & Processes
Your Day-to-Day

What Changes, and What Doesn't Yet.

The most common question after any big announcement is a practical one: what does this mean for how I do my job tomorrow? Here's the short answer.

Right Now
Keep working as you do today.
Continue using the systems, logins, tools, and processes you use today until you hear otherwise from your leader or People Operations.
Before Anything Changes
You'll hear about it first.
Changes to systems or processes will be communicated ahead of time, with clear timing and the training and support you need to make the switch.
Ongoing
Watch for updates.
Details on systems and processes will come through frequent internal communications. Zweig Group colleagues get the full walkthrough at the October 5 Live Orientation.
